Risk bands (LASA composite, 0–1 scale): High ≥ 0.75 · Medium 0.55–0.75 · Low < 0.55. "Highly similar" (POCA-style, 0–100 scale) follows FDA's published cutoff of ≥ 70.

About the POCA-style score: this reimplements POCA's published methodology (orthographic = mean of bigram similarity + normalized edit distance; phonetic = a Double Metaphone code-match, standing in for POCA's more complex ALINE algorithm). It is not FDA's actual software — the real tool is at poca-public.fda.gov, with source available only on request from FDA. Treat this as a triage aid, not a regulatory-grade result.
